Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

LifeTracker is a personal app that keeps track of regular payments (subscriptions, mortgage, insurance, utilities and other bills). It is run by its owner for personal use.

What is stored

  • Your sign-in email address.
  • The bills you add or confirm (name, amount, dates, notes), bank and card transactions (read-only copies), documents you upload, and your settings.
  • If you fill in “About me”: the names, emails, phone numbers, places and usernames to look for online; the web pages found about you; your mailing-list senders (address, name, counts, dates, the unsubscribe address, not the emails); documents that expire (kind, name, expiry date, last 4 characters of the number, an optional photo kept in your private storage); your credit-freeze checklist.
  • If you connect them: a Google sign-in (encrypted), and optionally a read-only bank link through SimpleFIN Bridge (encrypted). LifeTracker never sees your bank password.

Everything is stored in the app’s own Supabase database. Each account can only ever see its own data.

Google data

  • Google Calendar (calendar.app.created): the app creates one “LifeTracker” calendar and only adds, changes and removes events in that calendar (bill due dates, renewals, trial ends). It cannot see or change your other calendars.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, optional): the app looks for receipt, invoice and renewal emails to find bills. It also reads your banks’ purchase-alert emails (amount, shop, date) to find recurring bills, with simple patterns (no AI). It also reads breach-alert emails (Have I Been Pwned, Mozilla Monitor) to tell you which accounts were in a data breach, and the headers only (sender, subject, opened or not, unsubscribe header) of newsletters and ads to list your mailing lists. Only emails that look like bills are read in full; from them it keeps the bill details (company, amount, dates), not the email.
  • Manage mailing lists (gmail.modify, optional, only if you allow it): to send the short “unsubscribe” email some senders ask for, and to move emails from senders you unsubscribed from or blocked out of the inbox under a “LifeTracker/…” label. It never deletes email and sends nothing else.
  • Without that permission, the app never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • Email or document text may be sent to an AI service (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter or Anthropic Claude) only to read the bill or document details from it. Your “About me” details are sent to Google Gemini only to search the web for pages about you. Gmail data is never used to search the web or train AI models.
  • Ask LifeTracker: when you ask a question, a short summary of your own bills, spending, documents and dates is sent with it to the AI (free ones first), only to answer it.
  • Returns & warranties: order confirmations and receipts are found in Gmail; only those that pass a simple check are read by the AI, and only the shop, item, price and dates are kept.
  • LifeTracker’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared except with the services above to provide the app’s features.

Your choices

  • Disconnect your bank or Google at any time in Settings (you can also remove access at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access).
  • Download all your data in Settings → Account & data.
  • To delete your account and all its data, contact the owner below.

Contact
